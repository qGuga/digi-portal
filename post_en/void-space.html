The Void Space raid against Apocalymon is one of the most demanding End-Game encounters, requiring strict control over Tamer Skills, safe positioning against Healblock + DOT, and a synchronized group healing rotation.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 105k+ HP, 350%+ CT, 15.5k+ HT • Support: 105k+ HP, 34k+ DS, 350%+ CT, 15.5k+ HT • Tank: 230k+ HP, 60k+ DE, 215%+ BL, and 135.5%+ EV.

🛡️ Best PT Composition & Skills

  • Tank: 1x WarGreymon Shin (must bring 2x Protect).
  • DPS: 3x Omegamon Merciful Mode (OMM) (each DPS must bring 2 offensive Tamer Skills and 1 Emergency).
⚠️ General Mechanics (Entire Fight):
  • The Boss constantly casts Big Split and Blue Circles (Healblock) throughout the entire fight — including during Minion phases.
  • From 50% HP onward: Blue Circles begin applying a damage-over-time (DOT) + Healblock debuff that can quickly wipe players.

X → Emergency → Choco → HoH → HoH → Choco → Choco

Option 2 (Direct rotation):

🛡️ Special Attention for Tanks (After 35%):

After 35% HP, an invisible Feel the Pain hits immediately after every final Stack. Watch your HP and damage mitigations closely, as the hit lands even without a clear visual telegraph on the ground!
