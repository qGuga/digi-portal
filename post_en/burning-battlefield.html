The Burning Battlefield against Megidramon is a lengthy encounter packed with sequential mechanics involving Towers, Split/Stack circles, survival zones, and a lethal DPS Check during the final 10%.

⚠️ Note on Percentages: The triggers below are approximate and may vary slightly depending on your party's DPS speed.

🚨 WARNING AT 10% — FINAL PHASE (10% TO 0%)
  • Upon reaching 10% HP, a 1-minute countdown begins.
  • From this point on, defeat Megidramon AS FAST AS POSSIBLE.
  • If you fail to finish him within the time limit, the entire party will be wiped.
