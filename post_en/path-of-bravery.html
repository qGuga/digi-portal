The Path of Bravery against WarGreymon requires tight synchronization on the F3 Cooldown of your Hitters to skip heavy Stack and Split mechanics.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 85k HP, 310% CT, 13k HT • Tank: 120k HP, 25k DE, 130% BL, and 190% EV.

🛡️ Composition & Recommended Digimon

  • Hitter (DPS): Examon X or Fanglongmon Shin.
  • Tanker: Shoutmon DX, Amphimon, or MetalSeadramon Shin.
⚡ Opener (100% – 69%): Right at the start of the fight, all 3 Hitters must cast F3 as fast as possible so the skill comes back off cooldown around 60%, allowing you to skip the upcoming mechanic sequence.
