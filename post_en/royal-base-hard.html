The Royal Base Hard (RBH) is divided into 3 Rooms, with the third room featuring two back-to-back encounters against Death-X-mon and Alphamon Ouryuken Awaken (AOA).

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 80k HP, 330% CT, 14.5k HT • Tank: 150k HP, 30k DE, 165% BL, and 110% EV.

🚪 R1 — Room 1 (Initial Bosses)

🐉 Examon X (Exa)
  • 30% HP: Miko.
  • May cast Omae at some point.
  • Features Stack and Poison mechanics.
⚔️ Jesmon (Jes)
  • Portals and Stack mechanics.
  • Takes damage exclusively from DoT (Damage over Time).
  • Simply dodge mechanics.
👊 Gankoomon (Gankoo)
  • No critical mechanics.
  • Just dodge ground AOEs and burn him down.

🚪 R2 — Room 2 (Portals Area & DexDorugoramon)

  • Initial Gates: Quickly destroy the 2 low-HP Gates.
  • Left Portal: Short DPS Check against LordKnightmon X and Gallantmon X (they deal no damage — burn them fast).
  • Right Portal: Head back to the main room and enter the right Gate to face Room 2's final boss: DexDorugoramon.

🥚 Boss: DexDorugoramon

Recurring Mechanic (Smash the Egg): Occurs from the start to the end of the fight. Destroy the egg immediately as soon as the battle begins and whenever it respawns.
