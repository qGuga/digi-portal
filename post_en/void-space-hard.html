The Apocalymon Dungeon [Hard] (Void Space Hard) is one of the most complex raids on the server, splitting into simultaneous encounters against the Dark Masters (MetalSeadramon + Puppetmon and Mugendramon + Piedmon), deceptive screen messages, and coordinated duels against Housemon and MetalEtemon.

⚠️ General Elemental Debuff Rule: Whenever the Dark Masters spawn, check your debuff and attack the Boss OPPOSITE to your debuff. When the message "Be wary of the mark you carry" appears, swap targets immediately!

📋 MetalSeadramon & Puppetmon Phase Execution:

  • 2x Stack on MetalSeadramon: MetalSeadramon receives the Stack (used as the Stack anchor). The Tank must stack along with him.
  • Stack on Puppetmon: One of the DPS players will receive the Yellow Circle.
  • Mark Swap: When "Be wary of the mark you carry" appears, swap targets (Bosses).
  • Safe Zones & Housemon Sequence: After MetalSeadramon's Safe Zones, the following sequence occurs: GZN Circle → Yellow Split → Stack → Poison → Final Stack (~85k damage) → followed by the Housemon spawn (use him as the Stack spot if the PT agrees).
  • Phase 1 Finish: Pop Burst on Housemon as fast as possible, double-check your debuffs, return to your matching Boss, and clear the DPS Check.
💊 Recommended Rotation (Poison + 85k Final Stack) Big Yellow Split → Choco + Double HoH → Drop Poison far away → Choco → Move to Housemon → Chicken → Final Stack
🛡️ Tactical Tips — Phase 1:
  • Tip for the Tank: The Tank does not need to attack MetalSeadramon or grab his Aggro during this mechanic — this prevents getting the Yellow Split Circle and removes the need to stand in the Safe Zone.
  • 4-Split Mechanic: Pre-assign fixed directions with your PT beforehand (e.g., "I'm moving toward Tai" / "I'm moving to the opposite side"), just like in Pillars/Mugen DG Splits.
  • Watch Out for Chicken & Blue Circles: Do not pop Chicken immediately after the 1st Stack, as you will lose HP walking through Poison — save it for the final 85k Stack. While running to Housemon, dodge all Blue Circles to avoid getting Healblock (HB)!

🎭 2. Second Phase — Mugendramon, Piedmon & MetalEtemon

  • 50% HP: Blue Circle.
  • 45% HP: Stack.
  • 38% HP: Mugendramon and Piedmon spawn.
🗣️ Apocalymon's Deceptive Messages (Do the OPPOSITE! — 9 circles total):
On-Screen Message Actual Mechanic (What to do)
"I bet you'll be safe together" SPLIT (Spread apart!)
"Go on, spread apart" STACK (Group up!)

📋 Mugendramon & Piedmon Sequence:

  • Opener on Piedmon: Piedmon starts with Split followed by Stack. The 2 DPS on Mugendramon must also run over and stack on Piedmon.
  • Yellow Circle & Stack: Next comes Yellow Circle and Stack (everyone stacks on Piedmon). If a DPS gets the Yellow Circle, they should step away so the next Stack targets the Tank (if the Tank can solo-soak the Stack, everyone else can keep DPSing).
  • Mark Swap: When "Be wary of the mark you carry" appears, swap targets.
  • Emergency: Popping an Emergency around the 7th Split/Stack circle (or right after) is recommended if your HP or food items are running low.
  • Phase 2 Finish: After the final Stack on Mugendramon, the 2 DPS resume full damage (PUSH!).
🛡️ Note for Tanks (Evilmon): You can simply ignore Evilmon. He literally cannot damage you (all of his attacks will miss) — he is purely decorative.
🐒 Critical Mechanic: 4 MetalEtemons (Pink Circle — DO NOT HESITATE!):
  • Mandatory Buff: A small pink circle will appear beneath one player. It applies a minor DOT, but grants the only buff that allows you to damage MetalEtemon. Everyone must grab this buff (stack at the agreed spot, such as Mugendramon).
  • Clockwise Order: Four MetalEtemons will spawn — eliminate them in clockwise order.
  • Golden Rule (Stay Stacked!): Always remain stacked on top of MetalEtemon unless a Split is specifically called. Even if Etemon is at 1% HP or dies half a second early, keep stacking until the mechanic resolves and run together to the next one!
  • Visual Glitch: The pink circle may flicker and visually disappear, but it remains mechanically active. Do not hesitate!
  • 🚫 Warning for SK Players: DO NOT cast F4 outside of a Stack! If the pink circle spawns beneath you far from the party, your teammates will miss the buff and won't be able to hit MetalEtemon.

💀 3. Final Phase — Apocalymon (30% to 0%)

  • 30% HP: Returns to standard Apocalymon mechanics, including 3x Stack, Split, etc.
  • 10% HP: Stack with both Digimon and Tamer together so the Tank can survive and soak the final Stack.

🐛 Known Bug — Super Reflected Debuff (64% & 40%):

If multiple players are standing too close or stacked right when the Reflect shield activates (64% and 40%), you may get hit with the Super Reflected debuff, preventing you from hitting the Bosses properly. If this happens, leaving the PT and rejoining clears the debuff and lets you attack any Boss normally.
