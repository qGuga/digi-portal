The Neverland dungeon features straightforward, easy-to-follow mechanics, but requires attention to the flat damage from the Omae skill, a Digimon with strong area damage (AOE) to clear minions, and a DPS Check during the final 10%.

📊 Stat Recommendations (Patch Notes)

Stat Recommendation
HP 75k+ (not strictly mandatory, but the higher the better to tank Omae)
HT 10k+
CT 200%
CD 350%
BL 100%

⚔️ General Mechanics & Preparation Tips

  • Base Cycle: Throughout the entire fight, Splash Damage and Blue Circles repeat continuously.
  • Digimon Pick: Using a Digimon with low-Cooldown (CD) area skills (AOE) is recommended to clear minions quickly.
💥 Omae wa mou shindeiru (70k Damage):

This ability deals 70k flat damage to the target. To survive if you have lower HP, activate your Tamer Protect skill right upon impact.

📋 HP Triggers and Key Mechanics

Boss HP Triggered Mechanic
~78% Omae (70k damage — use Tamer Protect if needed).
50% Small Blue Circle: You must stand inside it or you will be wiped (KO).
~40% Minion spawns begin. Use AOE skills to clear them quickly.
40% – 10% Previous mechanics continue repeating.
10% – 0% ⏱️ DPS Check: Defeat the boss with maximum damage before the timer expires.
