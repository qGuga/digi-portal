The Piedmon Dungeon is one of the most technical End-Game raids, requiring precise control over summons (LadyDevimon, Evilmon, and Etemon), mandatory use of the UVF shield, and tight synchronization during the purple circle sequence in the final stretch.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 105k+ HP, 350% CT, 14.8k+ HT • Support: 115k+ HP, 34k+ DS, 350% CT, 14.8k+ HT • Tank: 200k+ HP, 45k+ DE, 190% BL, and 130%+ EV.
💡 Tactical Entry Tip: If the Tank enters the dungeon last, the Evilmon (at 60%) will always focus (aggro) directly onto the Tank, making arena control much easier!

🚨 Mechanics Requiring Special Attention

🦇 80% — LadyDevimon (Hard DPS Check)
  • Applies Silence and Return to the party.
  • The first player to hit her cleanses the Silence and Return effects.
👿 60% — Single Evilmon (Kiting)
  • One player must hold Aggro and kite around the entire map (Reminder: if the Tank enters the DG last, Evilmon automatically focuses them).
  • All other players continue attacking Piedmon and handling mechanics normally.
💥 55% — Multiple Evilmon (One Shot AOE)
  • Must be cleared with a single area attack (One Shot AOE).
  • If any survive: Piedmon will instantly recover HP (he will continue using skills, though he won't re-summon pets that have already appeared up to this point).
🛡️ 50% & 49% — Tank Buster (120k) & Tank Check
  • 50%: Deals nearly 120k damage and ignores standard damage reduction — the UVF shield is mandatory.
  • 49%: Piedmon begins dealing significantly higher damage and unleashes two consecutive Tank Busters.
🐒 40% & 35% — Orange Circles & Etemon
  • 40% (Orange Circles): Deal damage and root players in place.
  • 35% (Etemon): Mechanic similar to UVF — only Etemon can damage you while other mechanics proceed normally. Leave space clear for UVF.
Purple Circle Sequence (30% to 10% — Every 30s) Stack → Split → Stack → Stack → Split

At 10%, prepare for the final Tank Check (and possible DPS Check).
