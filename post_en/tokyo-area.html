With the previous steps completed, your account advances to the Tokyo Area — one of the most important regions on the server for finishing the main storyline, unlocking gear dungeons, and accessing advanced farming zones.

Recommended Prerequisites: Completed previous Main Quests, Tamer Lv 115+, and Digimon Lv 120+ with basic accessories (AT, CD, HT).

Stage 1 — How to Unlock Access to Tokyo Area

  1. Head to DATS Center and speak with the NPC (Richard) responsible for unlocking the Tokyo-Odaiba questline (check the main marker on your minimap).
  2. Complete the introductory dialogue quest to unlock teleportation to the Tokyo Area maps.

Stage 2 — Map Sequence (Odaiba, Big Sight, and Shibuya)

  1. Follow the main quest trail by defeating the initial mob groups and collecting the required quest items.

Stage 3 — Tokyo Area Dungeons

Players who chose the SK (Skill) role will need to run the dungeons in this area to craft the ADV (Advanced True Vice):

  • At Tokyo Tower Observatory, you will find the SkullMeramon DG, where you need to drop the item called SkullMeramon's Digicore.
  • At Fuji TV-Rooftop, you will find the Myotismon DG, where you need to drop the item called Soul of Myotismon.
  • In Odaiba, there are 4 dungeons for your SK Digivice: Venom (Normal), Bravery, Friendship, and Venom (Hard), which drop Venom Myotismon Venom, Crest of Courage, and Crest of Friendship, respectively.
  • After gathering all required materials to craft your ADV (Advanced True Vice), head back to DATS Center.
  • Locate the NPC Nanomon.
  • Open the DigiVice tab and craft the respective items until your ADV is complete.
