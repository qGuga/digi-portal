Fanglongmon Hard (FDGH) is the server's ultimate End-Game raid, requiring pre-assigned cardinal positions before the fight starts, strict survival against one-hit-kill (OHKO) poison puddles, and sharp DPS positioning.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 105k+ HP, 350%+ CT, 16.3k+ HT • Tank: 240k+ HP, 60k+ DE, 229%+ BL, and 135%+ EV.

🧭 1. Direction Assignment (Decide before starting!)

Direction Color Arena Reference
West ⚪ White Baihu side
South 🔴 Red Zhu side
East 🔵 Blue Qing side
North 🟢 Green Always reserved for the Tank

🚨 2. Mandatory Precautions & DPS Awareness

  • Poison (OHKO): Deals instant death — never step into poison puddles.
  • Dodge everything: Any ground circle (including Blue Circles and Yellow Meteors) can cause instant death.
  • Tamer Skills: Use at the start of the fight, at 50%, and again whenever off cooldown.
  • After respawning: Run back toward safety first and only then Digivolve (Evo).
  • DPS Positioning: Keep HP above 100k during the first phase and always stay behind the Boss (opposite side from the Tank) to avoid getting hit by FTP.
