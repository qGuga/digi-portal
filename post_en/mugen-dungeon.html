The Mugen Dungeon against Mugendramon features fewer early mechanics compared to Puppet Dungeon (PDG), but demands a rapid response at 49% HP and full synchronization during the final burst at 8%.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 100k HP, 350% CT, 14.5k HT • Tank: 170k HP, 40k DE, 185% BL, and 205.5% EV.
⚠️ Note: The percentages below are approximate and may vary depending on your party's DPS. Throughout the fight, roughly 3 to 4 Stack and Unstack sequences will occur.

📋 Chronological Mechanics Table (85% to 8%)

Boss HP Triggered Mechanic (Mugendramon)
85% – 80% Omae.
70% – 65% Stack (without Unstack).
60% – 55% Another Stack (also without Unstack).
49% The boss summons 2 minions that must be killed very quickly.
24% Blue Circles: Each player must stand inside a separate circle (1 per circle).
10% Keep up steady DPS until reaching the 10% mark.
8% 💥 Everyone must activate Max Power Burst!

⚠️ Special Alert — Minions at 49%

  • Upon reaching 49% HP, Mugendramon will summon 2 minions.
  • Eliminate them immediately: If the party takes too long, the boss receives a buff that drastically increases his attack damage (Nuke Damage).
  • Once the minions are cleared, switch DPS back to the boss.

🚨 Finale — 8% (Critical Moment)

🔥 FLAWLESS FINAL EXECUTION:
  • Upon reaching 8% HP, the entire party must activate Max Power Burst.
  • Heal Management: Time your healing items and skills accurately to sustain damage while finishing off the boss.
  • Zero room for deaths: If any player dies during this final stretch, the run will likely be lost.

🍀 Good luck on your runs!
