Abyss Floor 6 is a mechanical endurance fight where, starting at 90% HP, the boss initiates continuous 30-second cycles while weaving in complex Split and Stack sequences.

⏱️ Continuous Cycles (From 90% down to 10%):
  • Big Split Circles: Begin at 90% and repeat every 30 seconds.
  • HB Meteors: Also begin at 90% and repeat every 30 seconds down to 10% HP.
