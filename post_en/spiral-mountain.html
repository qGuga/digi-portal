After wrapping up the main events in Tokyo-Odaiba, the next major story continent is Spiral Mountain, home of the Dark Masters and the gateway to advanced server content.

🗺️ Unlocking the Continent

  • Mandatory Prerequisite: You must complete the entire Tokyo-Odaiba main questline, including defeating Myotismon and VenomMyotismon (in the quest "Crisis on Digital World" / VenomMyotismon Dungeon).
  • Transition NPC: After finishing the final main quest on the Big Sight / Odaiba map (speaking with the DigiDestined NPCs and Gennai at the end of the VenomMyotismon storyline), the following quest will send you directly to speak with Gennai and enter Forest of the Beginning with the storyline active.

🚪 Dungeon Entrance NPC Locations

Each map in Spiral Mountain houses the entrance NPC for one of the four Dark Masters' raids:

1. 🌊 Forest of the Beginning — Marine Dungeon (MDG) Access

This starting map of the continent is where you will find the NPC responsible for entering the Marine Dungeon (MDG).

2. 🃏 Forest of Marionette — Puppet Dungeon (PDG) Access

Moving forward into the forest of puppets, you will locate the NPC that grants access to the Puppet Dungeon (PDG).

3. 🤖 Underground City — Mugen Dungeon (Mugen DG) Access

In the underground sector of the city stands the NPC providing direct entry to the Mugen Dungeon (Mugen DG).

4. 🎭 Top of a Nightmare — Piedmon Dungeon (Pied DG) Access

At the summit of the mountain, you will find the NPC granting access to the Piedmon Dungeon (Pied DG).

⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTE — Do not do the final quest right now!

You do not need to complete the final quest in Top of a Nightmare at this stage. It requires defeating Piedmon to finish — which is a Mid / End-Game dungeon. Therefore, skip it for now: ignoring it will not block your progression at all, as the next map unlocked after that quest only serves as access to the Apocalymon (Void Space) dungeon.
