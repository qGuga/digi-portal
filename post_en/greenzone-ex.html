The Green Zone [EX] raid against Bagramon features well-telegraphed mechanics that are easy to spot, but demands strict reaction times and defensive skill management during the final stretch.

🛡️ Tank Requirement Reminder: As recommended by the Staff, this dungeon requires Shoutmon DX as Tanker (1.8M+ HP, 30k+ DE, and 140%+ BL).

1. Blue Circles

  • Immediately dodge the blue circles that appear on the arena floor.

2. Troopmon

  • As soon as Troopmon spawns, kite away from it for roughly 5 seconds until it despawns.

3. Poison

  • When marked with poison, drop the circles along the outer edges of the arena so you don't clutter the center where the fight takes place.

4. Circle Near Bagramon (One-Time Mechanic)

💀 INSTA-KILL ALERT:
  • Immediately after the second poison, a circle will spawn right next to Bagramon.
  • Step inside this circle immediately — otherwise, you will be wiped instantly.
  • This mechanic occurs only once during the entire fight. Afterward, Bagramon will deal significantly higher damage with his basic attacks (AA).

5. Repeating Mechanics & Heal Block Debuff

  • Mechanics 1 through 3 will repeat.
  • Double Threat: The second Troopmon will spawn at the exact same time as the blue circles.
  • Fatal Debuff: Getting hit by the blue circles during this phase applies a 30-second heal block debuff that prevents using recovery items, making survival nearly impossible.

6. Tank Busters Phase

  • Previous mechanics continue repeating, now woven together with Bagramon's Tank Busters.
  • Always keep your HP at 100% before taking the hit.
  • Heal yourself immediately after each Tank Buster so you don't get finished off by his follow-up basic attacks (AA).
  • Cooldown Management: You must save your F3 skill to survive the third Tank Buster.
🏆 END OF BATTLE After surviving the third Tank Buster, withstand Bagramon's basic attacks (AA) for roughly 15 seconds. Once that timer is up, he will be defeated and the dungeon is cleared!

🍀 Good luck on your runs!
