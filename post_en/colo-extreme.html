Rounds R1 through R6 of Colosseum Extreme feature the same Digimon from Hero mode rounds R10 to R15, but with major mechanical variations and a massive increase in boss damage.

⚠️ Note on Percentages: HP triggers for meteors, Stack, Split, Tank Buster, poison, and minions are not 100% fixed — they may vary slightly depending on your party's DPS speed.

🛡️ Mandatory Party Preparation (Tank & Hitters)

  • For Tanks: The Tank Buster deals 73.5k damage even when using Guardian / Provoke (level 3 or higher) combined with the Defender title.
  • For Hitters (DPS): Equipping the Defender title is recommended for rounds R5 and R6. Ideally, also equip a 2x HP Bracelet.
  • Party Support: Having 2 Encouragements in the party is strongly recommended to keep max HP buffed during rounds R5 and R6.

⚔️ R1: Millenniummon

  • 50% HP (Minions & Immunity): At 50% HP, he summons 2 minions that must be defeated. While they remain alive, Millenniummon is completely immune to damage.
  • Minion Visual Bug: Sometimes the minions may fail to appear visually in the arena. If this happens, keep hitting Millenniummon with Auto-Attacks (AA) only until he starts taking damage again — only then resume using skills.
  • Other Mechanics: Identical to Colo Hero R10 (falling meteors and alternating Stack and Split mechanics).
Mechanic Sequence (R1) Split → Split → Stack → Stack → Stack → Split → Stack → Split → Stack → Split

🔥 R2: Susanoomon

  • Hero R11 Base: Retains standard falling meteor and blue circle mechanics.
  • Giant Circle (40%, 20%, and 10% HP): Upon reaching these HP thresholds, one of the Hitters (DPS) will be marked with a HUGE damage circle.
  • Positioning: The marked player must immediately run to one of the corners of the arena to avoid hitting the rest of the party.

🌺 R3: Rafflesimon

  • Hero R12 Base: Follows the same pattern as round 12 in Hero mode.
  • Area Poison (~40% HP): The only difference is that around 40% HP, she summons a massive poison circle in the arena.

🐉 R4: Alphamon Ouryuken X

  • Hero R13 Base: Features the exact same mechanics as Colo Hero R13, simply requiring extra focus from healers and tanks as his attacks deal significantly more damage.

🐲 R5: Examon X

This round introduces the most mechanical changes compared to Hero mode. Watch out for his two main area (AOE) abilities:

  • Feel the Pain: Heavy area (AOE) damage.
  • Dodge This!: Mechanic requiring immediate movement/dodging.

👁️ R6: Ordinemon

The final round of Extreme requires tight party coordination to dodge combos of Dodge This! followed immediately by Tank Buster.

🚨 Critical Alert (55% and 35% HP): If all players get hit by Dodge This! at these thresholds, the stacked damage is lethal — even with Henry's Tamer skill active (30% damage reduction), the hit still deals roughly 40k damage.
