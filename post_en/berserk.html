The Berserk Arena requires full synchronization between both party teams to defeat Omegamon Alter-S and Omegamon Zwart simultaneously, strictly respecting the HP stop thresholds.

⚠️ Note on Percentages: The HP values below are approximate and may vary slightly depending on your party's DPS speed.

🛡️ Party Requirements and Preparation

  • Recommended Composition: 2 Tankers and 2 Hitters (DPS).
  • Mandatory Consumables: Special Chicken Combo for everyone and Dark Chocolate (essential for the Tanker).
  • Hit Rate (HT): Recommended 10k+ (ideally 11k+ to land 100% of hits).
  • Support Skills: Regen (High/Highest, level 3 or 4) and Defender for incoming damage reduction (High/Highest, level 3 or 4).
  • Recommended Titles: Equip defensive titles such as "Dawn of a New Era" or "Defender of Winter 2023" to mitigate boss damage.

⚔️ Initial Strategy & Tanker Picks

  • Mandatory Synchronization: Alter-S and Zwart must be brought down at the exact same HP percentage.
  • Focus on Zwart: Use Shoutmon DX as Tanker.
  • Focus on Alter-S: Use IcyMagnamon or Cosmic Susanoomon as Tanker.
  • Cross-Summoning: Defeating Alter-S summons a Zwart minion (and vice versa). After the first minion spawn, all subsequent mechanics begin repeating.
🛑 GOLDEN RULE — STOP DPS AT THRESHOLDS! Stop all damage at 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20% until both Bosses are at the exact same HP percentage!
Minions will spawn immediately afterward at 79%, 59%, 39%, and 19%.
