Mandatory Prerequisites: Completed all Shinjuku Main Quests, Tamer Lv 110+, and Digimon Lv 120+ with basic accessories equipped.

Stage 1 — How to Unlock Access to Digital Area

  1. After turning in the final main quest in Shinjuku (East), Digital Area is unlocked as you progress further in the Shinjuku storyline and speak with Takato.
  2. Accept the transition quest that unlocks the portal/teleporter to Digital Area.
  3. Finish the initial dialogue upon entering the map to register the teleport point and unlock local NPCs.

Stage 2 — Quest Progression and Regional Objectives

  1. Follow the Main Quest sequence inside Digital Area to unlock all sub-regions of the map.

Stage 3 — Reaching Zhuqiaomon's Resting Area

  1. Upon arriving here, you unlock access to the Final, Burning, and Chaotic Dungeons.
  2. In these dungeons, you will evolve your D-Ark into the Digivice corresponding to your chosen role (AA or TA).
  3. This Digivice can roll anywhere from 50% up to 100% (purely RNG) and can be evolved twice.
