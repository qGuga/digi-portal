This guide organizes your account progression by milestones. Exact quest requirements may change after server updates, so follow the quest icon on the map and use this page as a progression checklist.

Main Rule: From level 1 to 120, prioritize the main quests needed to unlock the next map. Leveling and item farming come second until your progression is unlocked.

Stage 1 — Levels 1 to 30: Initial Systems

  1. Complete the tutorial in Yokohama Village and accept all quests offered by the NPCs.
  2. Head to Village of Beginning and finish all local quests before moving forward.
  3. Equip your Digimon with basic gear and keep a stock of healing items and money for fast travel.
  4. Upon reaching Tamer Lv 30, check the requirements for the next questline and unlock the required progression.

Stage 2 — Levels 30 to 44: Entering File Island

  1. In Silver Lake, complete the main quests until the next area unlocks.
  2. Advance to Silent Forest as soon as the requirements appear and continue the same quest chain.
  3. Enter Lost Historic Site only after finishing the current File Island objective.
  4. Keep your Tamer and Digimon progression balanced: short farming sessions near a safe zone help prevent level walls.

Stage 3 — Levels 44 to 64: Mid File Island Region

  1. Keep leveling your Digimon up to Lv 44 and confirm the Tamer Lv 30 requirement.
  2. Complete the available File Island storylines in sequence. Only grind the minimum levels needed for your current objective.
  3. Upon reaching File Island Waterfront, find the NPC responsible for the next island quest and follow the main marker.
  4. After clearing Waterfront, advance to Infinite Mountain as Tamer and Digimon requirements unlock.

Stage 4 — Tamer Lv 64 or Higher: Server Continent

Server Continent begins after completing the required main quests in File Island. Confirm the unlock at the crossing NPC before going back to grind on older maps.

  1. Desert: Complete the main questline and use the map to prepare your Digimon for the next stage.
  2. Canyon: Advance once story requirements are met and keep your gear matched to monster levels.
  3. Pyramid: Finish the main progression and pave the way for later maps and the Odaiba saga.

Stage 5 — Levels 64 to 95: Preparing for Odaiba

  • Basic Gear Setup: Equip a Ring, Necklace, Earring, and Bracelet with useful stats for your role.
  • Currency: Save your Tera for evolutions, gear, and fast travel. Avoid spending everything on temporary items.
  • Seals: Farm the seals suited for your chosen role (AA, SK, or TA).
  • Seal Priority: AA: HP > AT > CT  |  SK: HP > SK > DS  |  TA: HP > DE > BLOCK.

Stage 6 — Levels 95 to 120: Tokyo-Odaiba

After clearing Server Continent and unlocking Tokyo-Odaiba, head to DATS Center and speak with NPC BanchoLeomon.

  1. Take advantage of active weekend EXP bonuses before spending time on regular maps.
  2. Form a Party with players around your level to maximize XP gain.
  3. Consider your Digimon ready for Lv 120 once you can comfortably farm on the corresponding map.

Side Goals

  • Western Village & Digimon Maze: Use them as Tera or item farming options whenever your main objective hits a wall.
  • Exclusive Dungeons: We strongly recommend running the Gizumon Trial DG to unlock server-exclusive evolutions such as Agumon, Lalamon, Gaomon, and Falcomon.
  • Rank SSS & Jogress: Participate in active events and compare the real cost before investing all your resources.
