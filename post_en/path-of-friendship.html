The Path of Friendship against MetalGarurumon is defined by stacking meteor debuffs that increase incoming damage by 20% and a mandatory DPS stop at 60%–59% HP.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 85k HP, 310% CT, 13k HT • Tank: 120k HP, 25k DE, 130% BL, and 190% EV.

🛡️ Composition & Recommendations

  • Hitter (DPS): Omegamon Zwart D, Omegamon Alter B, Lucemon X, or Death-X-mon Awaken.
  • Tank: Roughly 100k+ HP.
  • Critical Rate (CT): At least 300%+ CT is required to land critical hits consistently.

⚔️ Opening Phase — 100% to 63%

Boss HP Triggered Mechanic (MetalGarurumon)
100% – 81% Pure DPS.
81% 2x Single Meteor (applies a debuff increasing incoming damage by 20%).
76% • 1x Single Meteor (+20% damage taken debuff).
• 1x wave of medium Blue Circles (movement speed debuff).
• 1x Single Meteor (+20% damage taken) → followed immediately by Freeze to Death! (Tank Buster).
63% • Multiple medium Blue Circles (movement speed debuff).
• Immediately afterward, 1x Single Meteor (+20% damage taken debuff).

🛑 60% – 59% — WARNING: STOP ATTACKING!

❄️ NORTHERN IMPACT (RETURN DEBUFF)
  • Northern Impact: Applies Return Debuff to the party.
  • Simultaneous sequence: 2x Single Meteor (+20% damage) → multiple medium Blue Circles (slow) → 1x Single Meteor (+20% damage) → followed immediately by Freeze to Death! (Tank Buster).
⏱️ Important (Update): Wait until the debuff expires before resuming attacks! Older guides mention 30 seconds, but this mechanic was nerfed and the debuff now lasts only 10 seconds.

📋 Remaining Mechanics (56% to 29%)

Boss HP Triggered Mechanic
56% Multiple medium Blue Circles (movement debuff), followed by 1x Single Meteor (+20% damage taken).
47% Freeze to Death! (Tank Buster circle).
36% • Feel the Pain: Heavy ranged AOE damage (deals ~29k damage to the Tanker).
• Immediately afterward, 2x waves of 2 Meteors (2 players receive meteors, applying +20% damage taken debuff).
29% • Very small Meteor on Tank: Step out of it immediately.
• Immediately afterward, 2x waves of 2 Meteors (2 players targeted — +20% damage debuff).
• Immediately after, Freeze to Death! (Tank Buster).
