After reaching level 120 and completing the initial steps in Odaiba, your account's next major goal is to complete the Shinjuku Saga. Finishing this questline is essential to unlock advanced areas and key server systems.

Recommended Prerequisites: Tamer Lv 99+, Digimon Lv 120+ (preferably SSS rank or Jogress), and a basic set of accessories with Hit Rate (HT) and HP.

Stage 1 — How to Unlock Travel to Shinjuku

  1. Head to DATS Center and look for the NPC (Richard) responsible for the Shinjuku / Tamers questline (check the purple/main marker on your minimap).
  2. Complete the introductory dialogue quests to unlock access to Shinjuku (West Area) via the teleport terminal.
  3. Make sure you have free inventory space for quest items and passes handed out during the storyline.

Stage 2 — Shinjuku (West Area Day): First Quests

  1. Upon entering Shinjuku (West), strictly follow the sequence of Main Quests indicated by the local Tamers and NPCs.
  2. Complete all gathering and elimination tasks until you receive the quest that unlocks passage to the eastern side of the city.
  3. Here you will face the first challenges of the saga, where you will farm your D-Ark in the Vikaralamon DG.

Stage 3 — Shinjuku (East Area Day)

  1. Advance to Shinjuku (East) as soon as the main quest directs you and keep following the story marker.
  2. Finish 100% of the Shinjuku quest chain to earn titles, extra experience, and unlock access to the next farming areas and dungeons.

Stage 4 — Shinjuku (Western Area Night)

  1. Advance to Shinjuku (Western Night) once prompted by the main quest and keep following the story marker.
  2. Complete 100% of the questline to claim your titles, bonus EXP, and unlock the next farming zones and dungeons.

Stage 5 — Shinjuku (Eastern Area Night)

  1. Advance to Shinjuku (Eastern Night) when directed by the main quest and keep tracking the story marker.
  2. Finish 100% of the Shinjuku quest chain to earn titles, extra EXP, and unlock access to upcoming farming maps and dungeons.
  3. Following the same challenge format as the first map, you will find the Vajramon DG here, which is required to craft your D-Ark.

What to Do After Finishing Shinjuku?

  • Upgrade Seals & Accessories: Use your accumulated resources to boost your offensive stats and accuracy before entering the Mid-Game phase.
  • D-Ark Dungeons: Run both dungeons mentioned above to craft your D-Ark at Jijimon (DMW Village).
