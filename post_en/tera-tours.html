This guide compiles all information on the two scheduled Tours available in DMW: Spiral Tour and Forest Tour. Both are timed activities that allow you to farm Tera by defeating Raid Bosses, while also gathering essential materials for progression and trading.

🗺️ There are 2 main Tours: 1. Spiral Tour (focused on direct Tera + Blessings) and 2. Forest Tour (focused on Unique Data for Goggles/D-Ark + Tera).

1. 🌀 Spiral Tour

  • Prerequisites: Unlock Spiral Mountain by completing the main storyline: File Island → Server Continent → Tokyo → Spiral Mountain.
  • How it works: Starts right at minute XX:00 every hour (e.g., 12:00, 13:00, 14:00...). Each Channel lasts ~2 minutes and must be cleared in order: CH0 → CH1 → CH2 (~24 minutes total duration).

2. 🌲 Forest Tour

The Forest Tour is essential for obtaining crafting and upgrade materials for Goggles and D-Ark Vices.

  • Prerequisites: Unlock access to Digital Area Map → Forest Area Map (by completing the Shinjuku and Digital Area questlines).
  • How to Access: In Forest Area, head to Gekomon's Liquor Factory, speak with Gallantmon (Dukemon), and enter the instance. After your first entry, you unlock the direct Jump Booster shortcut: Forest Area - Tour.
  • Mechanics: Private instance with 1 fixed Channel randomly assigned upon your first entry (e.g., if you get CH9, you will always enter CH9). Each Boss lasts ~2 minutes and drops 300M.

🎁 Rewards & Spawns (Forest Tour)

  • Gold Bar: 300M per Raid (Total of 2.4T per Tour).
  • Unique Data: Chance to drop 1 to 3 units across the 8 spawns (7 Bosses, with one spawning twice) — used to craft/upgrade Goggles and D-Ark Vices.
  • Specific Seals: Orochimon (10 Seals), BanchoLeomon (10 Seals), and Beelzemon (25 Seals).
  • Raid Summon Card Lv3: Chance to obtain multiple Seals, Chocolates, Yggdrasil Fruits, and other items.
  • Alternating Spawns: Certain spots alternate between Mugendramon ↔ Chaosdramon and Beelzemon ↔ Megidramon.

3. 🔄 Sequential Tours (Spiral → Forest → Spiral)

During time slots where both activities overlap, you can combine parts of each to maximize profit without sacrificing either one completely:

⚡ Practical Example — 15:00 Rotation (Estimated Profit: 3.1T):
  1. 15:00 (Spiral Tour): Start at Forest of Beginning on CH0.
  2. 15:05 (Forest Tour): As soon as you finish Forest of Beginning, use the Jump Booster to Forest Area and run the Forest Tour until ~15:20.
  3. After defeating Zhuqiamon: Use the Jump Booster to head straight to Top of the Nightmare.
  4. Top of the Nightmare (Spiral Tour): You will still catch the Piedmon Raid on CH1 and complete the entire CH2!

When is it worth it? Mainly when you are already selling the materials you farm. If you still need specific materials for upgrades, running the full Spiral Tour (3.6T) without missing any Raids may be better.

🎯 Which Tour Should You Choose?

  • Spiral Tour: Best choice for securing the highest direct Tera payout and stacking Blessings.
  • Forest Tour: Excellent if you need Unique Data, Seals, and Raid Summon Card Lv3.
  • Sequential Tours: Ideal for players who no longer need crafting materials and want to optimize profit by combining both.
