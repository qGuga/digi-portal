The Puppet Dungeon against Puppetmon features three distinct phases, requiring sharp focus on clearing the corner Housemons to generate shields against lethal attacks and proper F3 management during the minion phase.

📊 Stat Recommendations

🔥 Hitter SK
  • HP: 90k+
⚔️ Hitter AA
  • HP: 90k+
  • CT: 330%+
  • HT: ~14.5k+
🛡️ Tank
  • HP: 170k+ • DE: 40k+
  • BL: 175%+ • EV: 205.5%
  • Title: Defender • Deck: Tank
💡 Extra Note:
  • MetalSeadramon Shin: Applies a -100% debuff and increases damage taken by 500% on the target.
  • Alternative (Lower HP): DPS players can cast F3 right at the start of the fight to skip some early mechanics.

⚔️ First Phase — Main Boss (94% to 60%)

Boss HP Triggered Mechanic
94% Tank Buster.
~90% Meteors begin (continue repeating throughout the fight).
84% Tank Buster.
~78% Multiple Blue Circles.
~74% Feel the Pain.
70% 💀 Map AOE (One Hit): "Defeat the Housemon to activate the safe zone". Kill one of the Housemons in the corners of the map.
⚠️ Warning: Wait for the attack to miss (Miss Hit Animation) before stepping out!
65% • Stack → Split → Stack → followed by Feel the Pain.
• Optional: DPS players can cast F3 after the 2nd Stack to skip down to ~50%.
~60% Multiple Blue Circles + another Meteor.

🏠 Second Phase — Housemon Minion (At 50% Boss HP)

Upon reaching 50% HP, the Housemon Minion spawns. Switch targets to it and watch for its mechanics:

Housemon HP Triggered Mechanic
94% Tank Buster + Feel the Pain.
70% Stack → Split → Stack → Split. (After this sequence, 1–2 DPS should cast F3 to prevent repetition).
50% Small Blue Circles: Stand inside them or the party will Wipe (1 person per circle, otherwise someone will die).
30% Omae AOE (80k damage). (At 31%, 1–2 DPS should cast F3 to skip this hit). Afterward, DPS Check down to 0%.

🔄 Third Phase — Return to Main Boss & Finale

  • Transition: As soon as the Housemon dies, a Tank Buster will hit. (Optional: DPS players can cast F3 right after the minion dies to skip percentages and Meteors).
Boss HP Final Mechanics (Puppetmon)
39% Feel the Pain → followed by Split Circles.
30% 💀 Map AOE (One Hit): Kill the second Housemon in the corner of the map.
⚠️ Wait for the Miss Hit animation! Immediately afterward, everyone receives Split Circles.
24% Poison Circle on 2 players → followed by Feel the Pain.
20% Split Circles → followed immediately by Small Blue Circles (1 person per circle or the party Wipes!).
⏭️ At least 2 DPS must cast F3 right after the Blue Circles to skip straight down to ~10%!
20% – 10% Repeating Blue Circles, Meteors, and Splits (skippable with high DPS).
10% – 0% Final ⏱️ DPS Check until Puppetmon is defeated.
