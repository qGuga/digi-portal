The Final Battlefield uses the Common Skip Run as its standard strategy, requiring tight coordination between Cosmic Susanoomon (F3 protection), the UlforceVeedramon X / UK player on Raguelmon, and the Alphamon Ouryuken X (OX) hitters during Skip windows.

⚡ 100% – 70% (Opener & Tank Buster)

  • 100% – 70%: Rarely triggers a DPS Check. Hitters should use all Tamer skills right from the start of the fight.
  • 70% HP: Emotional Damage! (Tank Buster circle). Between these mechanics, the 3 Hitters may also receive a Split Circle.

🛡️ ~63% to 60% — Stack & Confusing Rotations

  • Around 63%: Stack Circle — you must use Cosmic's F3 protection.
  • 63% – 60% (Variable Rotations): Different sequences of huge damage circles and poison circles begin in this HP bracket.
🔄 Most Common Rotation (63% – 60%):
  1. 2x waves of huge circles (4 circles each).
  2. 1x wave of 2 poison circles.
  3. 1x additional wave of 4 huge damage circles.
Other possible rotations:
  • Variation 1: 1x poison (2 circles) → 4x huge circles (4 each).
  • Variation 2: 1x huge circles → 1x poison → 3x huge circles.
  • Variation 3: 3x huge circles → 1x poison → 1x huge circles.
  • Variation 4 (Very rare): 4x huge circles → 1x poison.
⏭️ Skip Trigger: Right after the fourth huge circle, the OX hitters must use both All Delete skills to skip the boss's HP down to 50% or lower.

🚨 Raguelmon Spawn (Wipe Mechanic)

💀 DO NOT KILL RAGUELMON! (KILLING IT CAUSES A WIPE)
  • 13% – 10% HP: Stop attacking Raguelmon (the exact cutoff depends on your party's UK player).
  • UK Action: The UK player must use F3 to bring Raguelmon down to 4%–1% or 0% without killing it.
  • Protection Buff: Once Raguelmon hits this range, the party will receive protective buffs.
  • Immediate Stack: As soon as Raguelmon despawns, a Stack Circle will appear. If executed properly, the buff will protect the PT — no need to stack.

👾 48% – 47% (Minions & AOE Debuff)

  • 48% – 47%: Minion spawn occurs — stay away from Zhuq.
  • Right after the minions spawn, an AOE Debuff will go out.
  • OX Action: As soon as the minions appear, quickly use All Delete (if anyone gets hit by the debuff, it will be cleansed).
  • Immediately Afterward: The 3 Hitters will receive a Split Circle.

📉 37% to 13% (Repeating Mechanics)

  • Around 37%: Emotional Damage! (Tank Buster circle).
  • 30% – 29%: Poison circles and huge circles (same mechanic as the 63%–60% phase).
  • Around 20%: Stack Circle — use Cosmic's F3 protection once again.
  • Around 13%: The 3 Hitters will receive a Split Circle.

🐉 11% – 9% — Final Phase: Devas Spawn

⚡ FAST EXECUTION ON DEVAS:

Both OX hitters must rapidly use All Delete as soon as the Devas spawn, following this exact order:

  1. Antylamon
  2. Makuramon
  3. Lion

⚠️ If delayed: Multiple circles will rapidly spawn back-to-back, applying a Movement Debuff (Stuck) and heavy damage that can wipe the run.

🍀 Good luck on your runs!
