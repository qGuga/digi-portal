The Marine Dungeon requires at least 81k+ HP for Hitters to survive 80k flat damage attacks (Omae and Stack Circles), along with coordinated F3 timing for the Common Skip Run strategy.

📊 Stat Recommendations

🔥 Hitter SK
  • HP: 81k+ (required to survive the 80k Omae hit).
⚔️ Hitter AA
  • HP: 81k+
  • CT: 330%+
  • HT: ~14.5k+
🛡️ Tank
  • HP: 150k+ • DE: 30k+
  • BL: 165%+ • EV: 200%
  • Title: Defender • Deck: Tank
🛡️ Alternatives to Reduce Incoming Damage (If short on HP):
  • Defensive Titles: Master of the Ocean, Defender of Winter 2024, Holy Knight Disciple, Master Trickster, or other damage-reduction titles.
  • Memory Skills: Equip Defender on your Digimon to mitigate the 80k damage hits.
