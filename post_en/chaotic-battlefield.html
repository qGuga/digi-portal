The Chaotic Battlefield against Beelzemon uses the Skip Run as its standard strategy (skipping mechanics through coordinated burst damage from 3 Alphamon Ouryuken X).

⚠️ General Note: All HP percentages below are approximate and may vary depending on your party's DPS speed.

⚡ First Phase — 100% to 48% (Burst & Meteors)

  • 100% – 81/74%: Initial maximum Burst window.
  • 77% – 75% (Skip Trigger): The Tank Buster appears between 81% and 74% (most commonly between 77% and 75%). As soon as the circle spawns, all 3 OX must use All Delete simultaneously to skip the upcoming mechanics.
  • Large Meteor: If timed properly, all 3 OX will each receive a massive individual meteor.
  • ~61% HP: All 3 OX receive a smaller meteor (varies by DPS).
  • ~57% HP: 2 to 3 smaller meteors spawn. If party DPS is very high, there will be zero delay between the first and second meteors.
  • 50% – 48% HP: The Tanker receives a Tank Buster circle once again.
🔄 Possible Variations in This Phase:
  • Beelzemon may summon 2 smaller meteors before the Tank Buster and 2 after it.
  • He may skip the meteors after the Tank Buster completely.
  • Most common scenario: 3 meteors before the Tank Buster and 1 after.

🎯 Second Phase — ~44% (Meteor & Stack)

  • Around 44%: Beelzemon summons a smaller meteor.
  • If this mechanic gets skipped by high DPS, he will cast only the Stack circle.
  • Most common sequence: 1 smaller meteor followed immediately by the Stack circle.

🚨 Third Phase — 40% (Critical Minion Mechanic)

At 40% HP, Beelzemon begins summoning 3 types of minions: SkullSatamon, IceDevimon, and Impmon. This is the hardest part of the dungeon and where most runs wipe.

💥 IMPMON ONE-SHOT RULE:

Each OX must eliminate their Impmon in a single hit (One Shot)!

Minimum requirements for One Shot: 2x Vigor OR 1x Vigor + 1x Focus.

📋 Execution Order for the 3 OX:

1st OX: Wait for the first and second Impmon to spawn and cast All Delete as fast as possible (if delayed, the boss's damage reduction will ruin the run).
2nd OX: One-shot the third Impmon as quickly as possible. After the second All Delete, ideally stop attacking once Beelzemon reaches 11% HP.
3rd OX: One-shot the fourth Impmon immediately.
🎯 Final All Delete Timing (11% → 7%):
  • Ideal scenario: The final All Delete hits Beelzemon right at 11%, skipping his HP straight down to roughly 7%.
  • Off-timing: If it lands before or after 11%, the burst will only bring the boss down to ~9% HP, making the final stretch much harder.

🏁 Final Phase — 7% to 0% (DPS Check)

  • The remainder of the fight acts as a short DPS Check against the clock.
  • The party has enough time to finish Beelzemon off, but the window is tight — go all-out without dragging the fight.

🍀 Good luck on your runs!
