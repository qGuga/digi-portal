The Shopping Mall (Mall Dungeon) features straightforward, repetitive mechanics, requiring focus only on the survival circles in the final stretch and managing the arena space where poison pools are dropped.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: Overall: 60k+ HP, 200%+ CT, 7.5k+ HT, 350%+ CD, and 100% BL.

⚔️ Core Mechanics

  • Base Cycle: Throughout the entire fight, Meteors and Poison mechanics repeat continuously alongside the Omae skill.
💀 SURVIVAL CIRCLES (30% AND 10% HP)

Upon reaching 30% and 10% HP, a small circle will spawn in the arena. Step and stay inside it immediately — otherwise, you will be wiped instantly.

🧪 Extra Note — Poison Stacking (4k/tick)

  • Long Runs: If party DPS is low and the fight drags on, do not try to stack all poison pools in the exact same spot.
  • Damage per Tick: Each poison puddle deals 4k damage per tick.
  • Safe Limit: Stacking poison in one corner only works up to a certain threshold — for instance, 10 poisons stacked in a single spot deal 40k damage per tick, easily wiping the party. Spread the poisons along the edges if the fight takes too long.

🍀 Good luck on your runs!
