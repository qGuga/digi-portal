The Bagramon Domain is divided into two straightforward boss encounters. While the mechanics are easy to grasp, it requires quick item usage at the end of the first fight and strict skill control (avoiding AOE) during the final phase of the second boss.

📊 Staff Stat Requirements Reminder: DPS: 80k+ HP, 265%+ CT, 350%+ CD, and 10k+ HT • Tank: 100k HP, 20k DE, and 100% BL.

👹 First Boss

  • Opening Combat: The first boss is straightforward. He will summon 2 pets — ignore them at first, keep attacking Bagramon, and wait.
  • Pet Multiplication: Those 2 pets will spawn additional minions (just like Dexmon in Royal Base Hard). Clear these newly spawned pets using AOE skills.
  • Top 1 DPS Item: As soon as the boss dies, the game automatically grants a special quest item to the #1 DPS on the damage meter.
  • Clearing the Room: The player who received the item must use it on the remaining pets to open the path forward.

😈 Second Boss

1. 50% HP — Lilith Summon

  • Upon reaching 50% HP, the boss will summon Lilith.
  • Switch targets immediately and focus her down.
  • ⚠️ Watch Out for Return: At a certain point during the fight, Lilith will cast Return — stay alert so you don't lose time or positioning.
  • After defeating Lilith, resume attacking Bagramon normally.

2. 30% HP — Invulnerability Phase (NO AOE ALLOWED!)

🚫 DO NOT USE AREA SKILLS (AOE)!
  • At 30% HP, the boss summons an initial pet. Kill this first pet and wait for the others to spawn.
  • At that moment, Bagramon gains an invulnerability buff (takes zero damage).
  • Once all pets appear in the arena, eliminate them one by one (Single Target).
  • Do not use AOE! Defeat each pet individually until the boss's invulnerability shield disappears.
🏆 FINISHING THE RAID As soon as the last pet drops and the invulnerability buff fades, simply finish off Bagramon to clear the dungeon!

🍀 Good luck on your runs!
